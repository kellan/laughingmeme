I was supposed to ride the [NYC Century today](https://nyccentury.org/), but I ended up coming down with a nasty cold and I'm spending the day on the couch.  

I enjoy the [Outlanders 2 game](https://www.pomelogames.com/outlanders2/), an easy going village sim. But somehow it's never quite acquired one of those fandom wikis with the various details, and sometimes I'm curious about how many days it takes carrots to mature and what their yield is. (usually when playing in Tourament mode).

I used it as an excuse to try two LLM backed work modes I haven't tried much, computer use and decompilation (really data mining of assets, no actual decompilation happened). Computer use was slow and finicky, and I hadn't consciously realized that you can't use your computer for anything else while the LLM is driving it. Mining the games assets by contrast worked better and was easier than I could have possibly imagined.

So using the information Sol extracted from the game files, I put together a quickly [Outlanders 2 "wiki"](http://outlanders2wiki.com/). (wiki in the sense that there is a repo that will accept pull requests vs "wiki" in the sense that you can click to edit).
